Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

SnoreTalk (by TieChan Software) listens while you sleep and shows how much you snored and when you talked in your sleep. We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, phone number or location. Google AdMob, which shows the ads, does collect some data — see Advertising below.

Your recordings stay on your phone

The microphone is used only after you tap Start, and only until the session stops (when you hold Stop or at the stop time you chose). While the app is listening it shows a notification, so you always know it is recording. Sounds are analysed on your phone by a small sound-recognition model built into the app; no audio is ever uploaded to us or to anyone else. The app keeps short clips of sleep talk, snoring and other sounds, and — only if you turn on Full-night recording — the whole night as audio files.

Data stored on your device

For each night the app keeps a summary (times, minutes of snoring, loudness estimates), the clips described above, and any factors and notes you add. It also remembers your language and settings. All of this is saved only in the app's private storage on your phone. A clip, a full-night file, a backup or a night summary leaves your phone only when you choose to share or save it — for example with the Android share sheet, or by saving a backup file to a folder you pick. The night summary used for "Who's snoring?" contains times and numbers only, never audio.

Recording other people

Only record people who know and agree. Laws about recording others differ between countries; you are responsible for using the app only where and with whom it is allowed.

Permissions

Microphone: to listen for snoring and sleep talk after you tap Start. Notifications (Android 13 and newer): to show the listening notification with a Stop button and to tell you when your report is ready; the app still records if you refuse. Foreground service (microphone): lets recording continue with the screen off until you stop it. The app cannot read your contacts, photos, location or other files.

Purchases

SnoreTalk Pro is sold through Google Play. Payment is handled entirely by Google Play; we never see your card, wallet or bank details. Google Play tells the app which Pro product your Google account owns, so the app can turn Pro on and restore it after you reinstall.

Advertising

The free version shows ads through Google AdMob: a banner at the bottom of the Report, Nights and Trends screens (never on the listening screen), one full-screen ad when you open a new morning report (not after your first two nights), and a short ad you can choose to watch to export a full night or to compare two phones. Pro removes all ads. To serve ads, Google may collect your device's advertising ID and technical information about your device. This is handled by Google, not by us. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads. Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

What Google may collect

An advertising identifier and app set ID (resettable IDs, not your name); your IP address, used to estimate your approximate location; app interactions, such as app launches, taps and ad views; and diagnostic and device information, such as device type, operating system version, app launch time and hang rate. Google uses this data to show and measure ads, for analytics and to prevent fraud, and it is sent over encrypted connections.

Users in Europe

If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the app first asks for your consent (using Google's consent form) before your data is used for personalised ads. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy. If you do not consent, you may still see ads, but they will not be personalised.

Data security

SnoreTalk has no server and no account, so we never receive or store your recordings or your data anywhere. Your recordings and settings are kept in the app's private storage on your phone, which other apps cannot read. Backup files you create are ordinary ZIP files and are not encrypted, so keep them somewhere private. The advertising data described above is collected and sent by Google's AdMob SDK over encrypted (HTTPS) connections and handled under Google's own privacy policy.

Data retention and deletion

Your nights and clips stay on your phone until you delete them in the app, clear the app's data or uninstall the app; each of these deletes them immediately. You can also let the app delete old clips automatically (Settings → Delete old clips; off by default). Full-night audio is kept for your 3 most recent nights. Files you shared or saved outside the app stay where you put them until you delete them. We hold no copy that would need deleting.

SnoreTalk Pro terms

Pro is offered as a monthly subscription or a one-time lifetime purchase. The monthly subscription renews automatically each month through Google Play until you cancel it in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions; cancelling stops the next renewal and Pro stays on until the end of the period you paid for. The lifetime purchase is a one-time payment that unlocks Pro for as long as the app is offered, on the Google account that bought it. Refunds follow Google Play's refund policy. We may change the price of the subscription in the future; Google Play will notify you in advance as its rules require, and a price increase never applies to a period you have already paid for. SnoreTalk is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any medical condition; its numbers are estimates from sound recorded by your phone and can be wrong. If you are worried about your sleep or breathing, talk to a doctor. Only record people who know and agree.

Children

This app is rated for users 13 and older and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.

A versão gratuita mostra anúncios do Google AdMob: um banner na parte de baixo das telas Relatório, Noites e Tendências (nunca na tela de escuta), um anúncio de tela cheia quando você abre um relatório novo de manhã (não depois das suas duas primeiras noites) e um anúncio curto que você pode escolher assistir para exportar a noite inteira ou comparar dois celulares. O Pro remove todos os anúncios. Para exibir anúncios, o Google pode coletar o ID de publicidade do seu aparelho e informações técnicas sobre o aparelho. Isso é feito pelo Google, não por nós. O que o Google pode coletar

Um identificador de publicidade e o ID do conjunto de apps (IDs redefiníveis, não o seu nome); seu endereço IP, usado para estimar sua localização aproximada; interações com o app, como aberturas, toques e anúncios vistos; e informações de diagnóstico e do aparelho, como tipo de aparelho, versão do sistema, tempo de abertura do app e travamentos. O Google usa esses dados para exibir e medir anúncios, para análises e para prevenir fraudes, e eles são enviados por conexões criptografadas.

Usuários na Europa

Se você está no Espaço Econômico Europeu, no Reino Unido ou na Suíça, o app pede primeiro o seu consentimento (com o formulário do Google) antes de usar seus dados para anúncios personalizados. Você pode mudar sua escolha a qualquer momento em Configurações → Privacidade de anúncios. Se você não consentir, ainda poderá ver anúncios, mas eles não serão personalizados.

Segurança dos dados

O SnoreTalk não tem servidor nem conta, então nunca recebemos nem guardamos suas gravações ou seus dados em lugar nenhum. Suas gravações e configurações ficam no armazenamento privado do app no seu celular, que outros apps não conseguem ler. Os backups que você cria são arquivos ZIP comuns e não são criptografados, então guarde-os em um lugar privado. Os dados de publicidade descritos acima são coletados e enviados pelo SDK do AdMob do Google por conexões criptografadas (HTTPS) e tratados segundo a política de privacidade do Google.

Retenção e exclusão de dados

Suas noites e clipes ficam no seu celular até você excluí-los no app, apagar os dados do app ou desinstalá-lo; qualquer uma dessas ações os exclui na hora. Você também pode deixar o app apagar clipes antigos automaticamente (Configurações → Apagar gravações antigas; desligado por padrão). O áudio da noite inteira é guardado para as suas 3 noites mais recentes. Arquivos que você compartilhou ou salvou fora do app ficam onde você os colocou até você apagá-los. Não temos nenhuma cópia que precise ser apagada.

Rekamanmu tetap di HP-mu

Data yang disimpan di perangkatmu

Untuk setiap malam, aplikasi menyimpan ringkasan (jam, menit mendengkur, perkiraan kerasnya suara), klip di atas, serta faktor dan catatan yang kamu tambahkan.
